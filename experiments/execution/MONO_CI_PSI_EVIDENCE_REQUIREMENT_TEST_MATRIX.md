## Verification entry points

- `make test-mono-ci-b0-psi-execution`
- `make test-mono-ci-psi-transition`
- `make development-quality`
- `.github/workflows/mono-ci-psi-transition-development.yml` runs the fresh isolated execution, archive packaging, and archive readback on hosted Linux.

The coverage/necessity report is a DEVELOPMENT diagnostic. `RETAIN_CANDIDATE`
or another technical label does not establish a formally justified scientific
decision: exact pair adequacy, normative applicability, historical baseline
identity, independent review, and authorization remain separate requirements.
