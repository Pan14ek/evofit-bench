## Confirmed evaluator capability and limits

EVAL-01 and EVAL-02 are controlled W2 checks of static evaluators on canonical A0.4, not Method P or MONO-E1 validation.

For the A0.4 observations, both evaluator source artifacts are pinned by digest `5c17373ba8896ef0c443d826bd0064168ec7c5759c7973f9602b6c2c715b169c`; `AC_PUBLIC_API` config SHA-256 is `8853c3a933b23f698c7ba7c5b6e511f292e8d9285dc0de4378741c53e466f7bb`, and `AC_MODULE_GRAPH` config SHA-256 is `1abae6871a477ff2a07685ad645a70defa2f1ac6745598ae4cdc35aab4e95bf6`. The A0.4 observation digests are `3b894993e088b5ac73a74f1e6c6c410ebcfe9fcbdf2a73cbe551689e2433e351` and `31c3cf5ca864f3f47ff37ae50db068ab792bb863d5be0c7e73b54266fbd766da`, respectively, in the [canonical checkpoint](../architecture/acceptance/a1/a0_4_canonical_checkpoint.json).

- **[EVAL-01](../experiments/pilots/EVAL01_REPORT_V1.md):** the finite preregistered cases yielded `F1=1.0` for `AC_PUBLIC_API` (1 TP, 5 TN over 6 evaluable cases) and `AC_MODULE_GRAPH` (3 TP, 3 TN over 6). Three technical calls per case were collapsed; 42 evaluator calls are not 42 independent cases. Missing-package scope was `NOT_EVALUABLE` and excluded.
- **[EVAL-02](../experiments/pilots/EVAL02_REPORT_V1.md):** ten source conditions yielded `F1=1.0` for `AC_PUBLIC_API` (4 TP, 4 TN over 8 applicable cases) and `AC_MODULE_GRAPH` (2 TP, 4 TN over 6 applicable cases). These are finite, author-selected, applicable cases only; there is no population estimate, confidence interval, or independent semantic review.
- EVAL-02 recorded three diagnostic-expectation mismatches for one `AC_PUBLIC_API` condition, one per repeat: the binary `NON_COMPLIANT` result matched, but output included `UNDECLARED_PUBLIC_INTERFACE` in addition to the preregistered `PUBLIC_INTERFACE_MISSING`. All 60 calls completed with zero launch, parse, or provenance failures. Preserve the mismatch and nonzero execution status as recorded.
- The post-run distinction is recorded in the [EVAL-02 erratum](../experiments/pilots/EVAL02_POST_EXPERIMENT_ERRATUM_V1.md); it does not relabel those mismatches as successes.
- The dynamic private-import condition is architecturally labeled `NON_COMPLIANT` but outside both evaluators’ direct-import inventory; both returned `COMPLIANT`, and the condition was excluded from binary metrics. The keyword-only signature break is outside the configured public API contract and remains `INDETERMINATE`. `AC_PUBLIC_API` does not check callable signatures; `AC_MODULE_GRAPH` does not detect dynamic imports, reflection, runtime injection, database permissions, raw SQL contents, or deployable topology.

Therefore byte-level source/evaluator readback establishes identity and evidence integrity only. Evaluator applicability, architecture compliance, representation semantics, and evidence-backed obligation coverage remain separate claims. Synthetic controls do not supply the missing independent exact-pair review.

## Ranked critical path

1. **W0 / policy authority / methods reviewer — settle authority and denominator.** Approve a versioned policy/registry, closure authority, A0.4 applicability and mandatory decision process, and lifecycle stages for all 18 IDs. Acceptance: every row has an authorized ACTIVE/INACTIVE/UNKNOWN and mandatory disposition with source facts/digests; closure is independently reviewed; no possibly mandatory unresolved item is silently omitted.
2. **W2 / W3 / independent semantic reviewer — establish required exact pairs.** Bind representation, obligation, stage, A0.4 source, evaluator/configuration, scope, and raw evidence. Acceptance: every required pair has non-vacuous positive and negative controls plus an independent semantic assessment; unsupported pairs stay UNKNOWN.
3. **P author / independent algorithm reviewer — freeze the measured P identity.** Resolve G1 decisions affecting method/operation semantics and freeze a general deterministic algorithm and contract. Acceptance: digest-bound executable/configuration/runner; generic obligation handling; UNKNOWN fails closed; scorer/oracle/`.git`/network/other-arm isolation is independently demonstrated.
4. **W0 / runner and fixture owners / fairness reviewer — freeze comparable arms.** Acceptance: immutable B0/P bundles and allowlists, same target source/workload/database fixture/environment, equal approved evaluator capabilities, image/source attestation, measurement boundaries, and explicit repetitions and missing/failure rules.
5. **W0 custodian / independent reviewer — assemble and freeze reference.** Acceptance: versioned reference contract binds universe, stages, pair evidence and completeness proof; scorer-only access controls and review route are recorded; one-time freeze and `load_verified_reference()` readback pass. Only then may W0 mark the checkpoint PASS.
6. **Independent methods review then authorized gate owner — complete G1 and separately grant G4.** Acceptance: OPEN-01…OPEN-09 receive actual review dispositions (OPEN-04/05 require external authority; the remaining open decisions require independent review), OPEN-10 remains deferred unless separately authorized, G1 is explicitly frozen, and G4 receives separate explicit measured-run authorization. Neither is inferred from this report or a CI pass.
