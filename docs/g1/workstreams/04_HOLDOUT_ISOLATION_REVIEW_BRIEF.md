## 1. Scientific-integrity objectives

The goal is to protect the independence of a future comparison among frozen B0, human B1, and generic Method P. P must be frozen before holdout predictions exist; method inputs must exclude target answers, private scoring logic, other methods’ outputs, repository history, and network access. A hash, passing test, or clean changed-path diff establishes only the property it actually measures.

A software negative probe can show that a particular synthetic test container denied particular attempts under one image and host. It cannot by itself establish that algorithm authors were blinded, that a selected future runner is isolated, that a human B1 participant was independent, or that a prediction was sealed before scoring. These are separate duties and evidence items.

This brief covers OPEN-09, the G2 evidence boundary, and the TT-P03-43 blocker. It can inform but cannot decide OPEN-01/02/04/05 or other review lanes. It does not amend frozen DA01 contracts. The source boundaries are described in the [G1 decision register](../../G1_METHOD_DECISION_REGISTER.md), [DA01 input-isolation contract](../../../experiments/execution/da01/INPUT_ISOLATION.md), [DA01 protocol](../../../experiments/execution/da01/PROTOCOL.md), and [readiness report](../../../experiments/execution/da01/READINESS.md).

## 5. G1 algorithm-freeze requirements

A G1 freeze is a human-governed record over exact versions and unresolved decisions. It is distinct from the technical fact that a branch or Docker image has a hash.

1. Resolve or formally disposition OPEN-01 through OPEN-09, including B1 cadence/exposure, manuscript/executable RQ mapping, endpoints and analysis, policy/evaluator authority, cost rules, scoring compatibility, and OPEN-09 custody. Keep OPEN-10/alpha-threshold adaptation deferred as currently scoped.
2. Independently review the generic algorithm against the method specification, invariants, allowed inputs, failure semantics, edge cases, and generic/adversarial fixtures. The reviewer records disagreements and limitations rather than silently editing a frozen DA01 contract.
3. Identify the exact P source commit, source tree digest, dependency lock, schema/API versions, configuration, and tests included in the review. Record the source/environment boundary and any generated files excluded.
4. Freeze method code and preregistered analysis before any target prediction. Any post-freeze modification creates a new version; it cannot replace a failed or unfavorable frozen result under the same version.
5. Freeze and hash the method-specific input contract and each future run bundle separately. Confirm that common B0/B1/P inputs and the declared P-only traceability are exactly scoped and reviewed for fairness.
6. Record reviewer independence, conflict and prior-exposure declarations, review artifacts, decisions, date/time, source SHAs, and outstanding blockers. Names/signatures remain blank until authentic participants complete the process.
7. Have the designated G1 authority record the gate disposition under its actual process. A reviewer recommendation, document packet, synthetic ACCEPT, green CI, or hash is not the G1 decision.
8. Keep measured-run permission closed. G4 is a separate later decision for a specific scope after selected-environment G2 and other prerequisites are evidenced.

## 6. Holdout custody and prediction-sealing procedure

This is a proposed workflow; do not execute it as part of this documentation task. It aligns with the DA01 protocol’s existing deterministic canonical-JSON and exact-byte-hash description.

1. **Appoint separate roles.** Identify protocol owner, independent reviewer, holdout custodian, B1 participant, scorer/operator, and G4 decision-maker. Record independence, conflicts, and prior exposure before data access.
2. **Resolve review decisions.** Freeze the method and protocol version only after independent review. Record OPEN decision dispositions and analysis choices; keep unresolved external authority explicitly pending.
3. **Inventory allowed inputs.** For each arm, create an approved manifest of relative path/role, source revision, version, byte count, SHA-256, and authority/provenance. The custodian checks that restricted roles are absent from method bundles.
4. **Freeze implementation.** Record P commit, dependency lock, image digest, launch command, runtime limits, configuration, and code/config hashes. B1 receives its own approved checklist/tool version.
5. **Verify the selected environment.** Run the G2 probes and input/output permissions on the actual selected runner and image digest; preserve test-specific output and environment metadata. Do not substitute a different local environment for a missing hosted image.
6. **Prepare isolated run directories.** Provide each method only its allowlisted input mount and an empty output mount. Use separate identities/workspaces, read-only inputs, no network, no checkout/.git, no inherited secret environment, and restricted output permissions.
7. **Execute without scoring access.** Capture command, source/bundle/image hashes, UTC start/end, environment identity, exit status, resource limits, and raw output/log hashes. Reject missing, stale, unexpected, or mutated inputs. Keep raw logs with the custodian if they may contain sensitive values.
8. **Validate and finalize the prediction.** Validate the method output against the frozen public output contract without consulting the oracle. A failed, held, or rejected run remains recorded as such; it is not rewritten into an accepted prediction.
9. **Seal exact bytes once.** Serialize according to the frozen canonical format, write to a new exclusive path, compute SHA-256 over the stored bytes, record byte length and UTC time, and sign a manifest with the responsible operator identity. The protocol says existing seal paths are never overwritten.
10. **Transfer to custody.** Transfer the prediction and manifest to independent write-once or append-only storage. The custodian acknowledges receipt and independently recomputes the exact-byte digest. Preserve access and transfer logs.
11. **Score only after all required seals.** The independent scorer verifies seals before consuming them and uses only the approved private reference process. Keep scorer inputs/results separate from method workspaces and release them only after the planned arms are sealed.
12. **Preserve deviations and result-informed changes.** Preserve invalid runs, failures, deviations, and unfavorable results. Any change made after score access receives a new code/protocol version and is labelled exploratory or evaluated on a fresh independently custodied holdout; never report it as the preregistered run.

A seal proves that the received bytes match the recorded digest and that a particular version was sealed at a recorded time under the chosen custody system. It does not prove that the algorithm was independent, that its inputs were complete, that the host was uncompromised, or that the prediction is scientifically correct.

## 8. TT-P03-43 independent verification plan

The source traceability row maps TT-P03-43 to P03-13: re-export pinned V1 B0/B1/P method inputs, verify V2/V3 and B1/P source parity, and establish legacy-byte compatibility plus identical common policy bytes. The traceability evidence names V1/V2 adapter tests and a B1/P shared-policy-and-common-Snapshot test, but says there was no pinned frozen method-output re-export or byte comparison. The completion/readiness record says a changed-path audit is not byte parity. The current count of 59 PASS and 1 BLOCKED does not convert the remaining row to PASS.

**Intended property, with scope caveat:** exact-byte parity is meant to establish that the re-exported frozen V1 B0/B1/P method-input bytes remain identical to their pinned references, while the specified V2/V3 compatibility checks and B1/P common-policy/Snapshot source parity hold for the exact versions under review. Byte equality detects serialization, ordering, line-ending, encoding, and accidental regeneration changes in the listed artifacts. It does not establish algorithm correctness, fair treatment beyond the named shared inputs, scientific validity, or secrecy. The TT row defines the categories and expected property but does not provide a complete relative-path inventory or authoritative version/commit list. The independent verifier must approve that inventory and the exact V2/V3 contracts before running the comparison. Do not expand it by inspecting restricted contents or treat DEVELOPMENT V2/V3 contracts as approved production authority.

**Protected verification procedure proposed:**

1. Have the authorized custodian identify the pinned V1 B0/B1/P method-input artifacts, their frozen source commits and exporter version, the exact V2/V3 contract versions to check, the B1/P common-policy and Snapshot artifacts, and the permitted re-export procedure. Record each relative artifact identity and role without copying contents into this brief. Do not open oracle, scorer, ground truth, expected operations, protected tests, or sealed predictions in this workstream.
2. Record a baseline manifest for the frozen V1 B0/B1/P inputs and a re-export manifest from the designated independent process. Separately bind the V2/V3 compatibility checks and B1/P common-policy/Snapshot comparison to their exact source revisions and bytes. Bind each record to source commit/tag, tool version, command, relative artifact identity, role, byte length, and digest. Retain raw manifests under the custodian’s access controls.
3. Independently recompute exact-byte digests from both sides and compare byte count and digest for every approved artifact. Where policy permits, use direct byte comparison as well as digests; never normalize whitespace, line endings, field order, or encoding before comparison.
4. Check that the tested exporter/tool is the approved version, the source resolves to the frozen commit, and no content was regenerated from a mutable working tree. Record failures, missing files, scope exclusions, and tool/runtime versions.
5. Produce a signed, dated verification record with inventory version, number checked/matched/mismatched/missing, aggregate manifest digest, method/tool versions, custodian and independent verifier roles, and deviations. Keep artifact contents and any sensitive per-file digest list in restricted custody. A public brief may state only approved artifact identifiers, counts, aggregate result, manifest digest if approved for release, and the review-record reference.

**Independent role:** a frozen-artifact verifier who did not author the exporter, implement the P changes under review, or control the relevant method outputs. The holdout custodian controls access and records transfer; a separate verifier checks bytes and signs the result. If the same person must hold both roles, a second independent witness must validate the manifest and access log under an approved process.

**Status transition:** TT-P03-43 may move from BLOCKED only when (1) the exact inventory and source revisions are explicitly authorized, (2) the independent comparison is completed on the frozen bytes without prohibited-content exposure to method authors, (3) every required object matches or a reviewed discrepancy is resolved under an authorized contract change, and (4) a reproducible evidence record is retained and independently reviewed. A diff-path scan, synthetic parity fixture, schema-valid output, hosted job pass, or this plan cannot satisfy those conditions. This task does not perform the check; status remains BLOCKED.
